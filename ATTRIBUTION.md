# Attribution

## Dictionary source

The dictionary entries in `packages/data/entries/` were reproduced on 2026-11-07 from the
**Treppecore** archive, "il vocabolario della lingua abruzzese", published by
[abruzzo.fm](https://www.abruzzo.fm/archivio/treppecore/treppecore.html).

The abruzzo.fm homepage states its reuse terms as follows (Italian, verbatim):

> Sei libero di riprodurre tutto il nostro materiale, basta citarne la fonte.

That is, all material may be reproduced provided the source is cited. This project cites
abruzzo.fm in the site footer, on every entry page, and in each entry file's `source.url`.

Written confirmation from the site owner that the dictionary may be redistributed under
CC BY-SA 4.0 has been requested. The reply will be recorded here when received.
