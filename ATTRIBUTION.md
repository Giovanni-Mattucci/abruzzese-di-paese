# Attribution

## Dictionary source

The dictionary entries in `packages/data/entries/` were reproduced on 2026-11-07 from the
**Treppecore** archive, "il vocabolario della lingua abruzzese", published by
[abruzzo.fm](https://www.abruzzo.fm/archivio/treppecore/treppecore.html).

The abruzzo.fm homepage states its reuse terms as follows (Italian, verbatim):

> Sei libero di riprodurre tutto il nostro materiale, basta citarne la fonte e se ne trai vantaggi
> economici, non ce ne frega niente.

That is, all material may be reproduced provided the source is cited, including for commercial
use. This project cites
abruzzo.fm in the site footer, on every entry page, and in each entry file's `source.url`.

### Written permission

On 2026-11-09 the abruzzo.fm editors confirmed by email that this project may publish their
content (Italian, verbatim):

> nessun problema, sei libero di pubblicare i nostri contenuti.
> Basta citare la fonte e magari anche Radio Abruzzo, unica radio al mondo di solo musica e
> contenuti abruzzesi

That is, the content may be published provided the source is cited, and they ask that Radio
Abruzzo be credited as well. The request described the corrections, the English translations,
community edits through this repository and the CC BY-SA 4.0 licence. The reply does not name
the licence; together with the homepage statement, which allows commercial reuse, it is the basis
for publishing the data under CC BY-SA 4.0. The maintainer keeps the original email.

### How the source is credited

- abruzzo.fm and its Treppecore archive: in the site footer, on every entry page and in each
  entry file's `source.url`.
- Radio Abruzzo, at the editors' request: abruzzo.fm is the Radio Abruzzo channel, and the about
  page credits it by that name with a link to https://www.abruzzo.fm/.
